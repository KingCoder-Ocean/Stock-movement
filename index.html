Stock movement classifier

Upload a stock movement export to see which products are fast, medium, slow or not moving, split into dry, chilled and frozen. The file is read in this browser and isn’t uploaded anywhere.

Adjust what counts as a pick and where products are stored

Storage area

The tool decides each product’s area in this order: your own choice in the Storage column, then the column below if its value is set, then the product name (for example tofu and beansprouts are chilled, prawns and dim sum are frozen, rice and sauces are dry). Anything it still can’t place goes to “To check”.

Storage list

Every product’s area is shown in the Storage column of the main table. Change any that are wrong there; your changes win over the guesses and are remembered in this browser. Download the list to keep a copy or share it, and load it back on another computer.

Movement types counted as picks

Tick the types that are sales or picks going out. Untick stock adjustments, transfers and write-offs so they don’t inflate the counts. Types that are mostly negative are ticked to start with.

Thresholds

Within each storage area, products are ranked by how often they’re picked. Fast covers the first share of all picks, medium the next share, slow the rest.
